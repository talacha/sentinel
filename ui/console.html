Sentinel console

Checking…

Static checks are free and instant. Live checks contact the model endpoint and Tavily (a few tokens and one search) to prove the keys work and the model exists.

Changes apply immediately and are saved so they survive a restart. Secrets are write-only: they are never shown again. Every change is recorded in the audit log (field names only).
